# Setting up email@ilmkhona0.com.co with Zoho Mail (Free)

Goal: a real inbox you can log into, send and receive from, on your own
domain — at no cost. Roughly 20 minutes of work + waiting for DNS.

---

## Step 1 — Create your Zoho account (you do this)

1. Go to **https://www.zoho.com/mail/** and click **Sign Up Now**.
2. Choose the **Forever Free Plan** (scroll down — it's under
   "Forever Free Plan", up to 5 users, 5GB each). Don't pick a paid plan.
3. Select **"Sign up with a domain I already own"**.
4. Enter your domain: **ilmkhona0.com.co**
5. Create your Zoho login + password and verify your phone number.

You'll land on the **Domain Verification** page. Leave it open — it shows
the values needed for Step 2.

---

## Step 2 — DNS records in Cloudflare

You'll add four kinds of records. Zoho shows the exact values; here's what
each is and the standard values for the `.com` data center (most signups).

### 2a. Verify domain ownership (TXT)
Zoho gives you ONE of these methods — easiest is the TXT record:
- **Type:** TXT
- **Name:** `@`  (or `zb...` code Zoho shows — use exactly what Zoho gives)
- **Value:** the `zoho-verification=zb******.zmverify.zoho.com` string
  shown on screen
Add it, then click **Verify** in Zoho.

### 2b. MX records (so mail can be received)
Add all three. In Cloudflare, **turn OFF / grey-cloud** is not needed for
MX — just add them:

| Type | Name | Mail server   | Priority |
|------|------|---------------|----------|
| MX   | `@`  | mx.zoho.com   | 10       |
| MX   | `@`  | mx2.zoho.com  | 20       |
| MX   | `@`  | mx3.zoho.com  | 50       |

> Important: remove any OLD MX records pointing at other mail providers
> first (your domain currently has leftover MX records from earlier).

### 2c. SPF (so your mail isn't marked spam)
- **Type:** TXT
- **Name:** `@`
- **Value:** `v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all`

If you already have an SPF TXT record, don't add a second one — merge the
`include:zoho.com` into the existing one.

### 2d. DKIM (signs your outgoing mail)
Zoho generates this per-account, so copy it from Zoho's DKIM page:
- **Type:** TXT
- **Name:** `zmail._domainkey`  (Zoho will tell you the exact selector)
- **Value:** the long `v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=...` string from Zoho

Then click **Verify** for DKIM in Zoho.

---

## Step 3 — Create your mailbox & test

1. In Zoho, finish setup and create your address, e.g.
   **ilmkhona@ilmkhona0.com.co** (or admin@, hello@, etc.).
2. Log in at **https://mail.zoho.com**.
3. Send a test email to your Gmail, and reply back from Gmail to confirm
   receiving works.

---

## Timing
- MX (receiving): usually works within 1–2 hours.
- SPF / DKIM: 4–48 hours to fully propagate (often much faster).

## Optional later
- **Forward to Gmail:** in Zoho settings you can auto-forward to
  ilmkhona@gmail.com if you'd rather read everything in one place.
- **Send-as from Gmail:** Gmail → Settings → Accounts → "Send mail as" →
  add the Zoho address using Zoho's SMTP (smtp.zoho.com, port 465, SSL).

---

### Standard Zoho values (quick reference)
```
MX:   mx.zoho.com   (priority 10)
      mx2.zoho.com  (priority 20)
      mx3.zoho.com  (priority 50)
SPF:  v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all
DKIM: (generated by Zoho — copy from the DKIM page)
```
If Zoho put your account on the EU or India data center, the servers may be
`mx.zoho.eu` / `mx.zoho.in` and SPF `include:zoho.eu` / `include:zoho.in`.
Always use the values Zoho shows on your own screen.
